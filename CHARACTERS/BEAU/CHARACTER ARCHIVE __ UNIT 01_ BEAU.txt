Beau is a model employee processing intensive quarterly data streams under the bleak, endless hum of corporate fluorescent lights. Outwardly, he is the ultimate company man—his head a rigid, unyielding block of brutalist concrete, completely overflowing with relentless paper trails.
But beneath the heavy stone exterior beats the heart of a father on the most critical deadline of his career. To make it home to his daughter's whimsical birthday party, Beau must navigate the sprawling, subterranean industrial corridors of Unit 7 and conquer the vanishing horizon of a distant time clock. He is about to discover exactly what it takes for the cold armor of the corporate grind to finally melt away.
