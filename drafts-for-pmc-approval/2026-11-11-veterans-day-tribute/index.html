Dear Friends,

As many of you gather around the table with family, friends and loved ones, from the warmth of a Southern California autumn we pause this Veterans Day to offer our deepest thanks to the men and women who have served this remarkable country.

Although I was raised in Britain, I have had the privilege of calling America home for more than twenty three years. Days like today are a meaningful reminder never to take the freedoms, peace and community we share for granted, and we remain profoundly grateful to every veteran and military family whose dedication makes them possible.

If your Wednesday brings you out for a stroll through the Village or our online gallery, our Rancho Santa Fe showroom is open under our new seasonal hours from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM, and we would be delighted to welcome you in. Please contact us anytime if we may assist you with any piece in the collection as the holiday season approaches.

My warmest regards,
Melissa Chapman
Founder and Principal Curator|
